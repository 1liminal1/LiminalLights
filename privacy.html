Privacy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This site is run by Liminal Lights, a small maker business in Sydney, Australia. It's an information site for the Prism LED cubes. There are no accounts, forms or payments here — every purchase happens on Etsy, under Etsy's privacy policy.

What this site collects

We don't ask you for any personal information. We do use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram). When you visit, it sends Meta:

  • which pages you view, including which Prism model you look at
  • when you click a Buy on Etsy button, and for which model
  • technical details such as your IP address, browser, device and the page you came from

The pixel sets a cookie in your browser, and Meta may link this activity to a Facebook or Instagram account if you have one.

Why

Etsy doesn't let sellers measure interest on their listings, so this site is the only place we can see which models people are interested in. We use it to understand that, and to show ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who've visited. We don't sell your information to anyone.

Other services this site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security — see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests it from Google — see Google's privacy policy.

Opting out

  • Change how Meta uses your activity for ads in your Meta ad preferences.
  • Block third-party cookies, or use your browser's tracking protection or a content blocker — the site works the same without the pixel.

Meta's own handling of this data is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

Questions

Message us through our Etsy shop and we'll answer, including requests about what's been collected.
